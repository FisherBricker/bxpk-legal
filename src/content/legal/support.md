# Support

hours.

## Common questions

**I can't sign in.** Check you are using the same method you signed up with: email, Apple or Google.
For email accounts, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen. If you signed up with Apple's
private relay address, sign in with Apple rather than email.

**My changes aren't showing on my other device.** Changes sync when the app is open and online. Open
the app on both devices with a connection and pull to refresh. Changes made offline are kept on the
device and sent as soon as it reconnects.

**The weather card is empty.** Forecasts need a trip location set from the location search, and
come from the US National Weather Service, which covers the United States only.

**Route recording stopped with the screen off.** Allow location access "Always" for the app in iOS
Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

**The app crashed.** Crash reports reach us automatically. If it keeps happening, email us what you
were doing, your device model and your iOS version.

## Reporting content or an account

Report any post or comment from the flag icon on it, and block anyone from their profile or from
that same menu. If something needs attention faster, email us with a link or a screenshot.

## Your data

- **See what we hold, or get a copy:** email us from the address on your account.
- **Correct it:** edit it in the app, or email us.
- **Delete your account:** Settings → Delete Account, inside the app. It is immediate and cannot be
  undone.
- **Privacy rights requests** under California, other US state or Canadian law: email us. We respond
  within 30 days.

# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 13 August 2026**

This policy explains what Backpack Weight Tracker collects, why, and what you can do about it.
It is written to be read, not to be survived.

> This is a draft prepared from what the app actually does. Have a lawyer review it before you
> rely on it.

## Who we are

Backpack Weight Tracker ("the app") is operated by Fisher Bricker. Contact:
[support page](https://fisherbricker.github.io/bxpk-legal/support).

## What we collect

**Your account.** Email address and a handle, so you can sign in and other people can find you.
Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive
whatever that service passes us, which is usually an email address and a name.

**What you put in the app.** Gear, trips, loadouts, meal plans, itineraries, and the photos you
attach to them. Posts, forum discussions, comments and reactions. Your display name and profile
photo.

**Body measurements, if you enter them.** Height, body weight, birthdate and biological sex. These
are used for one thing: estimating how many calories a day of walking with a given pack weight is
likely to burn. They are stored separately from your public profile, they are never shown to other
people, and they are never sold or shared for advertising.

**Who you are connected to.** Your friends, the trips you share, and the gear you have claimed a
share of.

**A device identifier.** We use Apple's DeviceCheck to recognise a device that has previously been
banned. It cannot identify you personally and cannot be used to track you across other apps.

**Advertising data.** The app shows ads through Google AdMob. If you allow tracking when iOS asks,
AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to personalise them. If you decline, you see the
same number of ads, chosen without it. See [Google's advertising
policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## What we do not collect

We do not collect your location. We do not read your contacts, photo library, or health data from
other apps. We do not sell personal information.

## What other people can see

- **Your handle, display name and photo** are visible to anyone signed in. That is how search and
  friend requests work.
- **Your posts and comments** are visible according to the audience you choose when posting.
- **Your trips, gear and weights** are visible only to you, unless you share a trip with someone or
  post about it. People you share a trip with can see the gear on that trip and its weights.
- **Your body measurements are never visible to anyone else.**

## Where it is stored

Google Firebase, on Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit
and at rest. The app is currently offered in the United States and Canada only.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting an item deletes it. Deleting your account is covered below.

## Deleting your account

**Settings → Delete Account.** It is immediate and cannot be undone. It removes your profile,
handle, photo, gear, trips, meal plans, itineraries, friendships, invitations, your posts, and the
photos you uploaded.

Two things deliberately survive, and neither identifies you:

- **A comment of yours that other people replied to** stays as "Deleted user" with the text
  removed, so the conversation underneath still makes sense to the people who took part in it.
- **Gear you shared on someone else's trip** keeps its weight but loses its name and your identity,
  so their pack calculations do not silently change.

Reports you filed about other people are kept, because they are records about those people rather
than about you.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Your choices

- **Tracking.** iOS asks once. You can change the answer any time in Settings → Privacy & Security
  → Tracking.
- **Your data.** Ask through the [support page](https://fisherbricker.github.io/bxpk-legal/support) and
  we will tell you what we hold about you.
- **Deletion.** In the app, as above, or ask us.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app rather than quietly
updating this page.

Additions to the table, for your call

No token was changed. Three values are new, each labelled here so nothing slips in unseen:

  • Added: topo particle. Accent at 45% opacity in light and 30% in dark for resting points, full accent for points the pointer has moved. Reason: at the topo line's own 22% and 10%, single points one and a half pixels wide disappear; the lines themselves keep the exact token.
  • Added: closing band. The download section uses the dark ground and dark text tokens in both themes, so the light page ends on the app's spruce Topo field. Reason: the page needs a real close, and this is the app's identity rather than a new color. Its text measures 13.81:1.
  • Added: phone bezel #10140D in both themes, for the walkthrough's device frame only.

Type, spacing, radii

Jost for display (500 for headings, 600 for the wordmark), set slightly open at large sizes, following BRAND.md. Nunito Sans for text (400 body, 600 links and labels, 700 figures and data labels), with tabular figures on every number so weights line up. Hero contour field

The rings are the app's ContourPanel geometry exactly: 1.9 to 1 ellipses around two off-canvas centers, (0.22 w, 1.45 h) every 17 px and (0.95 w, -0.55 h) every 21 px. Points sit along each ring about 7 px apart and the ring is drawn through them, so the contour line itself bends.

  • Reach grows from 70 px with a still pointer to 320 px at full speed.
  • Push grows from a gentle 0.6 px/frame² to 6.0 px/frame² with speed, plus a drag along the pointer's direction, so a fast swipe leaves a wake.
  • Spring pulls every point home with light damping: a soft wobble, never a bounce.
  • iPhone: a tap sends a ripple out from the finger, a sideways drag bends the lines, and vertical swipes still scroll the page.
  • Lines under the headline stay quiet until disturbed, so the text stays easy to read. The loop sleeps once the field settles and pauses off screen.

Turn on annotations to see a live readout of speed, reach and push in the hero's corner.

Reduced motion

With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, the page is complete and fully visible from the first paint: the contour field is drawn once at rest and ignores the pointer, nothing slides or fades in, and the walkthrough screens change without transitions. Pinning stays, because it is layout rather than animation. Use Preview reduced motion above to see it. Without JavaScript all content is visible too; only motion is lost.

Open questions for approval

  • Headline. "Know what your pack weighs, every day of the trip." reuses the site's current description. Keep it, or write your own?
  • Closing band. Keep the spruce close in light mode, or end on the light ground?
  • Walkthrough length. Four steps are shown. Add a fifth for route recording, or keep it short?
  • Community. Sample posts are labelled as samples. Keep the section before launch, or hold it until real posts exist?
  • Pricing. Nothing on the page says free or paid, because nothing confirms either. Say something?
  • Support email. Still a placeholder on the support page.
  • Sample content. The trip, gear, posts, news items and dates are illustrations and will be replaced with real entries.

## Reporting content or an account

You can report any post or comment from the flag icon on it, and block anyone from their profile or
from that same menu. If something needs attention faster, email us with a link or a screenshot.

## Your data

- **See what we hold:** email us.
- **Delete your account:** Settings → Delete Account, inside the app. It is immediate and cannot be
  undone.
